# **Rad AI Transcriptionist Candidate Evaluation**

*This task is designed to evaluate prospective radiology transcriptionists on their technical competency, transcription skills, and adherence to written guidelines.  You have 72 hours after receiving your evaluation email to submit the completed task.  The expected time to complete is 15-30 min.*

# **Instructions:**

1. Access the Sample Audio Files folder listed above.  Open and download each of the 15 samples (download option can be found under “more file actions” or “...” button in the top right corner.)  
2. Using the Google Sheets link provided in your evaluation email, open the spreadsheet and transcribe each dictation sample in the appropriate field.  Transcribe as expected in a final radiology report using current AMA style guidelines.  Notes or observations can be included in the Notes column provided.  If you are unsure of a word(s), please transcribe \[?\] in place of the text you are unable to verify.  
3. When you are ready to submit your final transcriptions, reply to the evaluation email “completed.”  Any changes made to your work after this will be invalid and not considered as part of your evaluation.

*If you have any questions or issues accessing the audio files or Google link, please reach out to Sara Champion at [sara.champion@radai.com](mailto:sara.champion@radai.com).  Once your transcriptions have been submitted, you should receive an update on your evaluation and application status within 1 week.*
